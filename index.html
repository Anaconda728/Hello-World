Dig and chop for materials, hunt for meat and hide, and craft your way to a rifle. Watch your food bar. Cliffs can collapse, jackals hunt at night (they fear campfires), and vipers hide in the grass. Your world saves in this browser.

You need a keyboard and mouse to play.

Where to find things: coal and iron ore are in the stone underground, so dig into hillsides and cliffs. Sulfur (yellow) lies deeper, below about height 26. Clay (blue-grey) is under the soil around lakes and on lake beds. Timber comes from trees. Meat, hide and feathers come from animals.
