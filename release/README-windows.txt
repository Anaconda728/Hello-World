NEON SPRAWL  v1.1.0  (Windows desktop edition)
==============================================
A dark-neon cyberpunk city builder. Build an endless megacity from above, then walk or
drive its rain-soaked streets.

HOW TO PLAY
-----------
1. Right-click the zip > "Extract All..." and extract it somewhere like Documents or the
   Desktop. (Don't run it from inside the zip.)
2. Open the extracted folder and double-click "Neon Sprawl.exe".
   Tip: right-click "Neon Sprawl.exe" > "Send to" > "Desktop (create shortcut)".

FIRST LAUNCH: "Windows protected your PC"
-----------------------------------------
This build isn't code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn you the first time.
Click "More info", then "Run anyway". You only need to do this once.

No install, no admin rights and no internet connection needed. To uninstall, delete the folder.
Your saves are kept separately in %APPDATA%\Neon Sprawl, so they survive updates.

CONTROLS
--------
Build:  Left click place / drag    Right-drag orbit    WASD pan (Shift = fast)
        Q/E rotate    Mouse wheel zoom    1-9, 0, - build tools    N drone
        G grow    X demolish
Walk:   WASD move    Mouse look    Shift run    Space jump (twice = double jump)
        Left click fire    Right click aim    1-5 / wheel weapons    R reload
        Q dash    E talk    X optic overlay    M clear map marker    V summon car
Drive:  W/S throttle/brake    A/D steer    Space drift    Shift boost    C camera
        X optic overlay    F get out
Always: B builder    Esc menu (save, settings, quit)    H hide HUD    P quick save
Window: F11 or Alt+Enter toggles fullscreen

WANTED LEVEL
------------
Attacking civilians, robots or patrol drones makes you wanted. Lose the drones by
breaking contact (hide in a dark alley to go faster), or walk up to a Data Spire or
Chrome Clinic and press E to pay to wipe your record.

FIRST STEPS
-----------
- NEW WORLD > name your city > "Jack in".
- Lay roads (1), build homes (2) and offices (3) next to them. When the POWER stat turns
  red, build another Fusion Core (5).
- F to walk, V to drive, B back to the builder. E near a robot or gang member to talk.

IF IT RUNS SLOWLY
-----------------
Esc > Settings > Graphics quality: Low or Medium. Plug in your charger: laptops often slow
the graphics chip down on battery. Turn on the FPS counter in Settings to check.

BACKING UP / MOVING SAVES
-------------------------
Esc > "Export save file" writes a .json file (Documents by default).
On another PC, use "Import Save File" on the title screen.
