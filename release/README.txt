NEON SPRAWL  v1.1.0
===================
A dark-neon cyberpunk city builder. Build an endless megacity from above, then walk or
drive its rain-soaked streets.

HOW TO PLAY
-----------
1. Unzip this folder anywhere (Desktop, Documents...). Keep all the files together.
2. Double-click "Play Neon Sprawl.html".
   It opens in your web browser. No install, no internet connection needed.

Best in an up-to-date Chrome, Edge or Firefox. Safari works too.
Use a mouse if you can: the trackpad works, but building is easier with a mouse.

WANTED LEVEL
------------
Attacking civilians, robots or patrol drones makes you wanted. Lose the drones by
breaking contact (hide in a dark alley to go faster), or walk up to a Data Spire or
Chrome Clinic and press E to pay to wipe your record.

FIRST STEPS
-----------
- Click NEW WORLD, give your city a name, and press "Jack in".
- You start in an empty district with one Fusion Core. Lay roads (1), then build homes (2)
  and offices (3) next to them. Watch the POWER stat: build more Fusion Cores (5) when it turns red.
- Press F to walk the streets, V to drive, B to return to the builder.
- Press E near a robot or a gang member to talk to them.

CONTROLS
--------
Build:  Left click place / drag    Right-drag orbit    WASD pan (Shift = fast)
        Q/E rotate    Mouse wheel zoom    1-9, 0, - build tools    N drone
        G grow    X demolish
Walk:   WASD move    Mouse look    Shift run    Space jump (twice = double jump)
        Left click fire    Right click aim    1-5 / wheel weapons    R reload
        Q dash    E talk    X optic overlay    M clear map marker    V summon car
Drive:  W/S throttle/brake    A/D steer    Space drift    Shift boost    C camera
        X optic overlay    F get out
Always: B builder    Esc menu (save, settings, quit)    H hide HUD    P quick save

SAVES
-----
Cities save automatically into your browser's storage, plus whenever you choose Save in
the Esc menu. To back up a city or move it to another computer, use
"Export save file" in the Esc menu, then "Import Save File" on the title screen.
Note: clearing your browser's site data deletes saves that haven't been exported.

IF IT RUNS SLOWLY
-----------------
Open Settings (title screen or the Esc menu) and set Graphics quality to Low or Medium.
Plug in your laptop charger: many laptops slow the graphics chip down on battery.
You can turn on the FPS counter in Settings to see the frame rate.

IF YOU SEE A BLACK SCREEN OR "SYSTEM FAULT"
-------------------------------------------
Your browser needs WebGL (hardware-accelerated graphics). In Chrome/Edge go to
Settings > System and turn on "Use graphics acceleration when available", then restart
the browser. Updating your graphics drivers can also help.
